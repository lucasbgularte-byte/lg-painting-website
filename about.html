Lucas' story

Entrepreneurship came before painting.

Lucas Gularte was born and raised in Porto Alegre, Brazil. At 18, he started his first business and went on to run a bar and restaurant, learning first-hand about customers, teams, operations and the responsibility that comes with building a company.

When the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted that business, his career took a different direction. Painting and decorating became the next chapter, and after moving to Ireland he developed practical experience across residential work and larger industrial environments.

LG Painting was established in 2022. What began with residential decorating is now evolving into a contractor capable of serving residential, commercial and industrial clients while keeping the same attention to preparation and finish quality.

Industrial background

Experience beyond residential decorating.

Lucas' industrial experience includes lead-painter work on power-station projects involving gas lines, fuel tanks and steel surfaces.

The work included grinding and cleaning, protective coating systems, MEWP access, crew coordination, environmental monitoring including temperature, relative humidity and dew point, and Dry Film Thickness inspection.
