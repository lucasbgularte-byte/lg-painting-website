Industrial Painting & Protective Coatings

Quality that can be measured.

Coating performance depends on correct surface preparation, environmental conditions, application and quality control.

01

Surface Preparation

Cleaning, degreasing, mechanical preparation, corrosion removal, priming and substrate inspection.

02

Environmental Monitoring

Air temperature, surface temperature, relative humidity and dew point assessed where required.

03

Film Thickness

Wet Film Thickness during application and Dry Film Thickness after curing, checked against specification where required.

04

MEWP Access

High-level work planned with appropriate access equipment and suitably trained operators where required.
