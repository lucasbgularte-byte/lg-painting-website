Technical controls

What we measure and manage.

01

Surface Preparation

Cleaning, degreasing, mechanical preparation, corrosion removal, priming and substrate inspection.

02

Environmental Monitoring

Air temperature, surface temperature, relative humidity and dew point assessed where required by the coating system.

03

Wet Film Thickness

WFT can be checked during application to help control film build before curing.

04

Dry Film Thickness

DFT can be measured after curing and compared with the specified coating requirement.
